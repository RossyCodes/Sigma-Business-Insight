01 — The problem

Your data isn't the problem. Finding time to read it is.

Every week, you hit export. The CSV lands in a folder. And that's where it stays — because you're busy running the shop, and nobody ever taught you pivot tables.

That spike during Ramadan you felt? The export knows. The quiet week after school holidays? The export knows too. It's all sitting there — in columns called Tarikh, Jumlah and Produk — waiting for someone with an hour to spare.

Built for real exports, not perfect ones.

Sigma recognises Tarikh, Jumlah, Produk — or Date, Amount, Item — and maps them for you. If it guesses wrong, one dropdown fixes it.
